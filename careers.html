Frequently Asked Questions

What is the application process?
Our application process is simple: Apply online, attend a screening call, and participate in interviews. We aim to keep the process smooth and efficient.
Do you provide training for freshers?
Yes! We welcome fresh talent and provide comprehensive training to help you succeed in your role. Our experienced team will guide you every step of the way.
What are the working hours?
We offer flexible working hours with hybrid work options. Our core hours are 10 AM to 6 PM, but we value work-life balance and accommodate flexible schedules when possible.
Are there opportunities for growth?
Absolutely! We believe in promoting from within and provide clear career progression paths. Regular performance reviews and skill development programs help you advance in your career.
