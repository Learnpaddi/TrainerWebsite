Frequently Asked Questions

What training programs do you offer?
We offer a wide range of training programs including Career Skills Improvement, Soft Skills Development, Digital Literacy, and Industry Connect programs. Our courses are designed to bridge the gap between academics and industry requirements.
Who can benefit from your training programs?
Our programs are designed for students, fresh graduates, job seekers, and working professionals looking to enhance their skills and career prospects. We tailor our training to meet the specific needs of each participant.
Do you provide placement assistance?
Yes, we provide comprehensive placement assistance including mock interviews, resume building, and connections with our corporate partners to help our trainees secure suitable employment opportunities.
Can you customize training programs for our institute?
Absolutely! We work closely with educational institutions and corporations to design customized training programs that address specific skill gaps and meet organizational objectives.
